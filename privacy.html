MMWT: My Weekly Trainer

Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

MWT: My Weekly Trainer ("the app", formerly Muscle Week Trainer) is made by Ali Hajj Hassan, Cork, Ireland, who is responsible for your data. This page explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. Contact: .

What the app collects

  • Your email address, to sign you in with a one-time code, a password you choose, or your Google account. With Google sign-in, Google shares your name and email address with the app; the app never sees your Google password.
  • Your trainer card: name, gender, age, height, body weight, goal, training experience, language and units.
  • Health and fitness information you enter: workouts (exercises, sets, weights, reps, effort, notes), cardio minutes and calories, calories and protein eaten, body weight, and any painful or injured body areas you tick.
  • Your settings: gyms and their equipment, favorites, your own exercises, routines and reminder times.
  • Feedback you choose to send, and your email with it only if you tick "You may reply to me".
  • Crash reports: when something breaks, the error message, the screen, the app version and the language. They contain no workout data.
  • Anonymous weekly bests, only if you turn this on: see "Comparing with lifters your weight" below.
  • Purchases: the app is currently free. If paid features are added later, they will be sold through Google Play, which handles payment; the app never sees your card details.

Why it is used

Only to run the app for you: to save your log, show your reports, suggest moves that fit your gym, goal and injuries, keep your licence, fix problems and answer feedback. The legal basis is your contract with us (providing the app) and, for the health information you enter, your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time by deleting it or your account.

Comparing with lifters your weight

If you turn on "Share my best lifts anonymously", then to show you what people of your weight lift, the app sends, once a week, your best lift for each exercise (as an estimated one-rep maximum), the week, your sex and your body-weight class in steps of 10 kg (for example 80–89 kg). Your name, email, age, photos, notes, injuries and other workouts are not sent with it. Only lifts that pass the app's realism checks are sent.

Other users can never read your entries; only you can see your own. The app only receives a summary for each exercise and weight class: how many people took part, the top 5% level and rough steps (deciles), and only when at least 5 people are in that group, so no single person can be picked out. Entries are linked to your account in the database only so you can delete them, and are removed when you turn the setting off or delete your account.

This is off by default and only starts when you turn it on (on the Week screen or in Profile → Compare with lifters your weight). Your consent is the legal basis. You can turn it off at any time; turning it off deletes what was shared.

What the app does not do

  • No ads, no advertising trackers, no selling of your data. Other users never see your name or your own entries, only anonymous group summaries.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera or photos. Pictures you save or share are created on your phone and only leave it if you share them.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your phone; their content is not sent anywhere.

Where your data is stored

In a private database run by Supabase (supabase.com) in the European Union (Ireland), encrypted in transit and protected so that each account can only read its own data. Emails with sign-in codes are sent through Google's Gmail. Google Play processes subscriptions under Google's own privacy policy.

How long it is kept

For as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, workouts, settings and anonymous weekly bests are deleted from the database straight away; copies in backups disappear within 30 days. Crash reports and feedback without your account are kept until they are no longer needed to improve the app.

Your rights

You can see, correct and export your data in the app (Profile → Export my workouts), and delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account) or on the delete your account page. You can also ask for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion by email, and you have the right to complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

Children

The app is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Health notice

Suggestions in the app are general fitness guidance, not medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
